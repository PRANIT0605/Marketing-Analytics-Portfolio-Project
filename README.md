## 📌 Project Description:
- A full-stack marketing analytics portfolio project that combines data extraction, sentiment analysis, and business intelligence using Python, SQL, and Power BI.  
- This project simulates a real-world case using a fictional brand (ShopEasy) for educational purposes. All datasets and insights are created to reflect practical, business-relevant marketing challenges.

---

## 📌 Project Insights & Goals:

### ✅ Increase Conversion Rates:
- **Goal:** Identify factors impacting the conversion rate and provide recommendations to improve it.  
- **Insight:** Highlight key stages where visitors drop off and suggest improvements to optimize the conversion funnel.

### ✅ Enhance Customer Engagement:
- **Goal:** Determine which types of content drive the highest engagement.  
- **Insight:** Analyze interaction levels with different types of marketing content to inform better content strategies.

### ✅ Improve Customer Feedback Scores:
- **Goal:** Understand common themes in customer reviews and provide actionable insights.  
- **Insight:** Identify recurring positive and negative feedback to guide product and service improvements.

## 📋 Key Business Insights from the Project:

### 📈 Conversion Trends:
- Strongest conversions in January & February (driven by Ski Boots, Boxing Gloves)  
- Weakest in July–October; requires campaign optimization  
- Highest product conversion: Hockey Stick (15.5%)  
- Lowest: Swim Goggles (5.6%)

### 📣 Customer Engagement:
1) Engagement peaks in Q1 (February)  
2) Blog posts perform better than social media & video for reach  
3) Basketball & Football Helmets had best traction on social platforms  

### 🗣 Sentiment Analysis:
1) Average rating: 3.69 ⭐️  
2) Majority of feedback is positive (275 highly positive reviews)  
3) Negative reviews peak mid-year, possibly indicating shipping or quality issues  
4) Insights suggest seasonal adjustments and better follow-up on mixed feedback
